About

Forty years of teaching one thing: better golf.

Meet your instructor

Glen Davis

Glen has been in the golf school business since 1982, teaching out of Myrtle Beach for more than 37 of those years. Over that time his schools have built one of the best returning-student ratios in the business — students come back because the changes stick.

He's been nominated for Golf Magazine's Top 100 Teachers in America, voted "Best on the Beach" instructor by Myrtle Beach Golf Magazine every year since 2003, and his schools have repeatedly been named "Best Golf School on the Grand Strand." Alongside teaching, Glen has 17 professional wins of his own.

What ties it together is a simple approach: work with each student as an individual, not as part of a curriculum. That's why every lesson is private, and why so many students end up coming back for more.

— Glen Davis
